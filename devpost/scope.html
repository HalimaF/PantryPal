What Can I Cook?

A website that tells a homemaker what to cook today from what is actually at home, without repeating recent meals.

Status: approved · Markdown version (scope.md) is the canonical copy.

The Unique Kernel

“What should I cook today, from what's in the house, without repeating what we just ate.”
Remembering recent meals is what separates this from a plain recipe search.

Who It's For

Homemakers, especially mothers in middle-class Asian households: a daily “what to make today?” and a tight budget that must last the whole month. First real users: the learner's own family. Later: students in rented apartments abroad.

The Core Loop

1Sign up / log in
→
2Enter monthly groceries
(photo or text)
→
3Food suggestion
+ today's preference
→
4Dishes + recipes
→
5Remember what was eaten

No groceries entered? Step 3 asks for her preference and the items she has, then suggests dishes and recipes. Step 5 feeds back into step 3, so dishes don't repeat too often.

The Boundary

Now (in the proof of concept)
  • Sign up / log in
  • Grocery entry by photo, with a text option
  • Food suggestion with a daily preference (rice, bread, roti…)
  • Fallback when no groceries entered: ask preferences and items
  • Recipes for suggested dishes
  • Remember eaten dishes to avoid repeats
  • Website that works well on a phone
