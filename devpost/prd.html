What Can I Cook?

A website for homemakers that tracks the month's groceries and suggests what to cook today from what's at home, without repeating recent dishes.

Status: approved · prd.md is the canonical copy · Develops scope.md > The Core Loop.

The Journey

Home is the hub. Every other screen has a way back to it. The loop that makes the app useful: suggest → cook → log → stock drops → suggest again.

Explore Each Screen

Login / Sign up

One screen with a switch between logging in and signing up.

Checks
  • A new person can sign up and land on Home.
  • A returning person sees their saved data.
  • A wrong password shows a clear message.

Home dashboard

Top: Add groceries. Middle: “X days until next groceries (28th)” and the list of what is left, with a total. Below: Dish suggestion, Daily log, Profile.

Checks
  • Shows days from today to the next shopping date.
  • Lists each item's remaining quantity with a total.
  • Add groceries is above the dashboard.
Empty state

“No groceries yet. Add your monthly groceries to see your days until the next shopping and what is left.”

Your definition: days left means days until the next grocery shopping; the list lets mum estimate the rest.

Add groceries

By picture: photograph an item, the app names it, she enters quantity and weight (chicken, 1 kg). By text: type item, quantity and weight.

Checks
  • Photo gives an item name she can correct.
  • She sets the shopping date; the next one defaults to the same day next month.
  • Quantity and weight are saved with it.
  • The item appears in the inventory list.
Error state

Photo can't be recognised: say so and offer the text option.

Dish suggestion → Recipe

Questions: lunch or dinner, heavy or light, base (rice, bread, roti, noodles). If no groceries are logged, it first asks what she has at home.

Checks
  • Dishes use ingredients in stock.
  • Recently eaten dishes don't come back.
  • Suggestions follow the profile's eating habits.
  • Tapping a dish opens the full recipe with the making process.
Empty state

Nothing fits: a clear message suggests adding groceries or relaxing a preference.

Log today's dish & Daily log

She picks the dish cooked. The app asks how much of each main ingredient was used (an estimate), or guesses from a photo of the dish. She can adjust it.

Checks
  • After saving, stock goes down and the remaining list updates.
  • The Daily log lists each day's dish and inventory used, newest first.

Profile

Number of people with age and gender, country, and eating habits (less spicy, Chinese or Pakistani cuisine, men like desi food, kids like junk food).

Checks
  • Details are saved.
  • They change which dishes are suggested.

Look and Feel

Clean and simple, easy to understand at a glance, with some “kitchen” warmth. Phone-first: big buttons, little text, one-handed. Colours and fonts are left to the builder within that direction.

States That Matter

  • First use: empty Home pointing to Add groceries.
  • Persistence: accounts, inventory, profile and logged dishes are saved per user.
  • Accuracy: quantities are approximate, and the app says so.
  • Privacy: each person sees only their own data, including family ages and genders.

The Boundary

Building now
  • Login and signup
  • Home dashboard: days until next shopping and what is left
  • Add groceries by picture or text
  • Dish suggestion with preference questions and a no-inventory fallback
  • Recipe pages
  • Log today's dish with ingredient amounts, and the Daily log
  • Profile with family details and eating habits
  • Phone-friendly website
Deferred or later
  • Exact quantity tracking and predicting how long food lasts
  • Flutter mobile app (after the family test)
  • Other languages
  • “Use soon” priority and missing-item shopping list
  • Dietary rules (halal, vegetarian) — not chosen yet
  • Students abroad version
Non-goals
  • Native mobile app in this hackathon — website first for faster family testing
  • Exact inventory — approximate by design
  • A general recipe website
